## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment:** The homepage does not read as AI-generated. It avoids SaaS cream/beige marketing templates, neon gamer dark mode, tiny uppercase eyebrows, numbered section markers, hero metrics, and identical card grids. The composition is restrained and content-first, matching the "Midnight IDE" north star. The biggest slop-adjacent element is inherited from the global stylesheet: thick left-border accents on blockquotes and reply-tips, which fall under the absolute side-stripe ban.

**Deterministic scan:** The detector found 3 side-tab warnings in `assets/css/style.css` (blockquote 5px left border at line 344, reply-tip 4px left border at line 2029, and a commented-out hide-content 4px left border at line 2044). Additionally, 4 broken-image warnings fired in `page.php` and `post.php`, but these are false positives: they come from lazyload/fancybox regex replacement logic, not empty `<img>` tags.

## Overall Impression

The homepage is disciplined and readable. It succeeds at being quiet, fast, and content-forward. The main opportunity is voice and orientation: the page assumes the visitor already knows who the author is, and the hard-coded English section titles create a tonal mismatch for a Chinese-language blog. A few small content and accessibility upgrades would turn a "nice template" into a confident personal site.

## What's Working

1. **Content-first layout.** No hero banner, no marketing metrics, no oversized headline. The page leads with the author's notice/description and then the article list, exactly what a reader expects from a personal blog.
2. **Configurable modules without code.** Notice, social icons, and project recommendations are all controlled from the Typecho admin, matching the "配置即表达" principle.
3. **Responsive typography and spacing.** The system scales cleanly from mobile to desktop, with comfortable line length (max-width 825px) and readable body copy.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Author identity is under-signaled on the homepage.**
- **What:** The only author signal is the site title in the navbar and an optional Notice text. If Notice is empty, the hero area shows the site description, which may be generic.
- **Why it matters:** PRODUCT.md defines success as "访问者在 2 秒内感知到页面主题与作者身份." Right now a first-time visitor may not know whose blog this is.
- **Fix:** Add an optional author name/avatar block in `.my` section, controlled by theme options. Default to site title + favicon if unset.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable shape homepage-author-block`

**[P1] Hard-coded English section titles clash with the Chinese context.**
- **What:** `Latest Articles`, `Projects`, and `View More »` are hard-coded English strings on a `zh-CN` page.
- **Why it matters:** Breaks voice consistency and makes the theme feel imported rather than finished for its primary audience.
- **Fix:** Move these strings to configurable theme options with sensible Chinese defaults, or wrap them in Typecho `_t()` translation calls.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify index.php`

**[P2] Article list gives only date + title.**
- **What:** Each post row shows the month/day and title. There is no excerpt, category, or reading-time hint.
- **Why it matters:** Readers scanning the list cannot judge relevance without clicking through.
- **Fix:** Add an optional excerpt (1 line) and/or category tag beneath the title, controlled by a theme option so minimalists can disable it.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout index.php`

**[P2] "Source" button label is inaccurate for non-code projects.**
- **What:** Every project card links to a "Source" button, even when the project URL is a demo or product page.
- **Why it matters:** Mislabels the destination and can confuse visitors.
- **Fix:** Change the default label to "Visit" or make it configurable per project.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify index.php`

**[P2] Side-stripe borders in the global stylesheet violate the absolute ban.**
- **What:** `blockquote` uses `border-left: 5px solid var(--blockquote-left)` and `.reply-tip` uses `border-left: 4px solid var(--link-color)`.
- **Why it matters:** Thick colored side borders are a recognizable AI-generation tell and are explicitly banned.
- **Fix:** Replace with full-border containers, background tints, or leading icons. For blockquotes, a left accent bar ≤1px plus tinted background; for reply-tip, the existing icon already provides the accent—remove the thick stripe.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish assets/css/style.css`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer):**
- Lands on the homepage and sees "Latest Articles" in English while the rest of the content is Chinese. Unsure if the site is localized or still being set up.
- Sees a "Source" button on a project and assumes it leads to code, but it might just be a product page.
- Social icons are invisible by default; Jordan has no idea the author has a GitHub/Twitter unless the admin explicitly enabled and configured them.

**Casey (Mobile User):**
- The fixed mobile navbar takes up screen real estate but is usable. Article list items are thumb-friendly full-width rows.
- The back-to-top button is reachable, but there is no search or filter, so Casey must scroll the entire list.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent User):**
- The dark-mode toggle button contains only SVG icons with `aria-hidden="true"`; the `<button>` itself has no `aria-label`, so screen-reader users hear "button" with no purpose.
- Navigation links do not set `aria-current="page"` for the active page.
- The article list uses `<h3>` for titles, which is good structure, but dates are plain `<time>` elements without a `<time datetime="...">` attribute.

## Minor Observations

- The `View More »` link sits at the end of the article list but is small and right-aligned; it could be a more explicit section button.
- The `.my` section relies entirely on Notice/description text. A very long notice pushes the article list far down the viewport.
- There is no featured/sticky post mechanism; all articles are treated equally.
- The detector flagged many undocumented radius and color tokens. This is mostly because `DESIGN.md` was just generated and some legacy values (e.g., `5px`, `0.3rem`, `#ffec99`) predate it. A tokens cleanup pass would align the system.

## Questions to Consider

1. Should the homepage lead with an author identity module (name + avatar + one-line bio) by default, or stay purely content-first?
2. Is the hard-coded English a deliberate bilingual stance, or should the theme ship with Chinese defaults and offer optional English?
3. Would a one-line excerpt per article improve scanning, or would it violate the desired minimalism?
